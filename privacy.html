Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to games, websites, customer support, and other services operated by MATS ARC Gaming Studio that link to this policy (collectively, the “Services”). A specific game or feature may provide an additional notice where its data practices differ.

Information We Collect

Depending on the Service and your device settings, we or our third-party providers may collect:

  • Device and technical data: IP address, device name or type, operating-system version, app version and configuration, language, approximate region, advertising identifiers, and other technical identifiers.
  • Gameplay and usage data: game sessions, feature interactions, progress events, ad interactions, performance measurements, and similar analytics.

Information you enter into a game may be stored locally on your device unless that game states otherwise. We do not intentionally collect payment-card details through this website. App-store purchases are processed by the relevant platform under its own terms and privacy policy.
